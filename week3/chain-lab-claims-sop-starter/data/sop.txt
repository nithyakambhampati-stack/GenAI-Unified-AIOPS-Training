Health Insurance Cashless Claims SOP (synthetic training document, Version 2)

[S1.1] Scope
This SOP covers cashless claims for planned and emergency hospital admissions under retail health policies handled by the claims desk.

[S2.1] Pre-authorisation for planned admission
The hospital submits the pre-authorisation request at least 48 hours before a planned admission. The insurer responds within 4 hours of receiving a complete request.

[S2.2] Emergency admission
For an emergency admission the hospital intimates the insurer within 24 hours of admission. The insurer decides on the emergency pre-authorisation within 2 hours of a complete request.

[S3.1] Documents required
Every request needs the pre-authorisation form, a photo identity proof and the treating doctor's note. A claim above Rs 1,00,000 also needs the hospital's investigation report.

[S3.2] Waiting periods
The waiting period for pre-existing diseases is 36 months. The waiting period for named specific illnesses is 24 months. An initial waiting period of 30 days applies, except for accidents.

[S4.1] Room rent limits
The room rent limit is 1 percent of the sum insured per day for a normal room and 2 percent of the sum insured per day for an ICU room.

[S4.2] Co-payment
Where the policy carries a co-payment option, 10 percent of the admissible claim amount is borne by the policyholder.

[S5.1] Rejection
The reasons for a rejection are communicated in writing within 3 working days. The policyholder may ask for reconsideration within 30 days of the rejection.

[S5.2] Settlement
Payment to the hospital is made within 7 working days of receiving the discharge documents.

[S5.3] Grievance
A grievance that is not resolved within 15 days goes to the Grievance Officer. After that the policyholder may approach the insurance ombudsman.
