Home Loan Servicing SOP (synthetic training document, Version 3)

[S1.1] Scope
This SOP applies to floating-rate and fixed-rate home loans to individuals that are serviced by branches. Staff must quote this SOP when answering borrower queries.

[S2.1] Part-prepayment
The minimum amount for a part-prepayment is Rs 25,000, and one part-prepayment is allowed per month. No charge applies to part-prepayment on floating-rate loans to individuals. On fixed-rate loans a charge of 2 percent of the amount prepaid applies.

[S2.2] Effect of part-prepayment
The borrower chooses whether to reduce the EMI or reduce the tenure. If the borrower does not choose, the tenure is reduced. The change takes effect from the next EMI cycle.

[S3.1] Foreclosure statement
The borrower must request foreclosure in writing. The foreclosure statement is issued within 3 working days of the request. The statement is valid for 7 days.

[S3.2] Foreclosure charges
No foreclosure charge applies to floating-rate loans to individuals. On fixed-rate loans the foreclosure charge is 2 percent of the outstanding principal.

[S3.3] Release of documents
After the loan is closed, the original property documents are released within 15 working days. Delay beyond 15 working days attracts compensation of Rs 500 per day to the borrower.

[S4.1] EMI holiday
An EMI holiday of up to 3 months is available once during the tenure, on written request approved by the branch manager. Interest continues to accrue during the holiday.

[S4.2] Change of repayment mode
A switch from ECS to NACH needs form HL-7 and takes 5 working days to take effect.

[S5.1] Processing fee
The processing fee is not refundable once the sanction letter has been accepted by the borrower.

[S5.2] Escalation
A complaint that is not resolved within 10 working days is escalated to the Nodal Officer of the bank.
