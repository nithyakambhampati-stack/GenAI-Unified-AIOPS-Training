TERM DEPOSIT - PREMATURE CLOSURE TERMS (fictional, for training use)
3.1 A term deposit may be closed before maturity on a written request signed by all account holders.
3.2 If the deposit is closed within 7 days of opening, no interest is paid.
3.3 If it is closed after 7 days, interest is paid at the card rate for the period the deposit actually ran, not the period it was booked for, minus a penalty of 0.75 percentage points. The rate paid is never below 0%.
3.4 Closure of a deposit of 500,000.00 or more must be approved by the branch manager.
3.5 Partial withdrawal is not allowed; the whole deposit must be closed.
Card rates (per year): 7-90 days 4.00%; 91-180 days 5.25%; 181-365 days 6.10%; over 365 days 6.75%.
